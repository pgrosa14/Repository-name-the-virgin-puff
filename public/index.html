The Virgin Puff

Record the Journey. Relive the Pleasure.

In the world of cigars, the first puff—the virgin puff—is something to be respected. It is the moment of introduction, when flavor, atmosphere, and intention meet for the very first time. It sets the tone for everything that follows.

Travel offers us that same feeling again and again. When you step off a plane into a place your eyes have never seen before and take in the first breath of unfamiliar air, you experience your own virgin puff of that destination—an introduction to a new landscape, a new culture, and a new moment in time.

This website exists to capture those moments.

Here we document the routes we traveled, the places we discovered, and the stories that shaped our journeys, so that our children and grandchildren may one day retrace these paths, breathe that same air, and experience their own Virgin Puff in the places we once explored.

Because every journey, like every life and every story, begins with a first breath.
